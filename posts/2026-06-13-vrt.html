Why I bought VRT

The most direct way to own the power and cooling buildout.

VRTVertivPower & cooling
What it does
Power and cooling equipment for data centers
Move
Bought Jun 5
Watching
Orders and backlog

Vertiv is the most direct way to own the power thesis. It sells the electrical and cooling systems inside data centers: power distribution, backup power, switchgear, and everything from room air handlers to liquid cooling piped to the chips.

Liquid cooling is the growth engine. AI racks now draw more power than air can cool, and the industry is converting fast. For Vertiv that's added revenue on top of what it already sells: coolant distribution units, piping and heat rejection are products the air-cooled world never needed. Revenue per data center goes up, on new builds and on upgrades.

The quieter strengths matter too. A large installed base produces service revenue through any cycle. Its gear is written into the standard designs that contractors and cloud companies use. And it's neutral: whoever wins the chip race needs power delivered and heat removed.

The risks: orders track construction schedules, so any pause in the buildout hits bookings. The stock has already run a lot, so it assumes continued growth. And large electrical conglomerates are always competing.

I sized it toward core. This is the position I expect to hold longest and think about least.
