Three buys: ALAB, IREN and CRWV

Putting new cash to work after a pullback.

I put a good chunk of cash to work today across three names, using new money I added to the account this month. Each gets its own post soon. This one covers why I bought them together and how I sized them.

The reason was price. All three had pulled back into the entry ranges I wrote down in advance. When a price reaches the plan, I buy. I want buying to feel mechanical, because the days when good companies are cheap are the days buying feels worst.

The three ideas are related but different:

  • Astera Labs is a bet that every AI server needs more connectivity chips each generation, whoever makes the processor.
  • IREN is a bet that access to electricity becomes the scarce thing.
  • CoreWeave is a bet that demand to rent AI computing stays ahead of supply.

Astera is the core position because its business quality is the highest. The other two stay smaller because they carry debt and execution risk. Even after today, about a third of the account is cash.

What would make today a mistake is a real slowdown in AI spending: actual budget cuts from the big builders, rather than a scary headline.
