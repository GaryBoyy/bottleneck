Marvell is buying Lumentum, and I added to AXT

Two of my companies agreed to merge, both stocks fell, and AXT got cheaper after its best quarter.

Two of my companies agreed to merge this morning. Marvell will buy Lumentum for 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share, a deal valued at about $115 billion. Both stocks fell anyway: Lumentum about 19% and Marvell about 13%.

A takeover target fell because the deal is paid in Marvell stock, so when Marvell drops, the offer is worth less. Even so, Lumentum closed about 11% below what the offer is worth today. That gap says the market isn't sure the deal will close. It still needs shareholder and regulatory approval, and a deal this size will get a long review.

The news buried a strong quarter. Lumentum's revenue more than doubled from a year ago, it won a second major co-packaged optics contract backed by more than $5 billion in purchase commitments, and it guided to another big jump next quarter.

If the deal closes, my Lumentum shares become Marvell shares, and Marvell becomes my main way to own both custom chips and lasers. That makes the account more concentrated, but I'm holding both and letting the deal play out.

Adding to AXT

I also added to AXT today, after the stock fell about 21% since its report on Thursday. That report was its best yet: record revenue, up about 160% from a year ago and more than 50% from last quarter, a 56% gross margin and a real profit. The business is accelerating while the stock falls with every other optics name.

AXT is now my largest position at about 14% of the account. Cash is about 12%.
