My rules for selling

Written down in calm, before any order goes in.

My winners have grown into position sizes I never chose. Astera Labs is now about a fifth of the account and AAOI about a sixth. Size earned by price isn't the same as size earned by conviction, so here are my selling rules, written before any order goes in.

1. Size. When a position grows well past its target weight, the excess gets trimmed back to target. The thesis can be fully intact and the trim still happens.

2. Consensus. Every position was bought on a view the market didn't share. When that view becomes consensus, the edge is spent, even if the business keeps doing well. This matters most for cyclicals.

3. Better use. Money belongs where it's treated best. If fresher ideas offer more upside, funding them from stretched winners is the job.

4. Broken thesis. Sold immediately and completely, at any price. None of the other rules apply.

The plan is a staged rotation over the coming weeks: trim the biggest winners toward target, exit names that haven't earned their place, and move toward power, cooling and a few new ideas. Cash will rise before it falls.
