August review

A better month, new cash on the sidelines, and why I'm not spending it yet.

August was a recovery month. The account rose about 7%, ahead of the S&P 500 and the semiconductor ETF, which each rose about 3%. It's still about 18% below where it was on June 30.

Movers

Sandisk (+29%) and Micron (+16%) led as memory bounced. AAOI rose about 14% after its report. AXT ran up nearly 60% in two weeks and then gave all of it back, ending the month flat. The laggards were One Stop and TeraWulf, each down about 15%. My June speculative buys, One Stop, Planet Labs and POET, are now each about 40% below cost.

New cash

I added new money to the account after the July buying, so cash is about 28% of the account. I'm in no hurry to spend it. July showed me what happens when I deploy too fast, and the backdrop has gotten harder: the Fed sounds ready to raise rates, oil is back near $90, and the 10-year Treasury yield is close to 4.8%.

The plan for September

  • No new positions until after the Fed meeting on September 16.
  • Add to core names only on big drops, in small steps.
  • The speculative tier stays frozen: no adds to One Stop, Planet Labs, POET or UAMY.
