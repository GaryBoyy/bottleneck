Why I added to ALAB

More connectivity chips in every AI server, whoever wins the processor race.

ALABAstera LabsChips & networking
What it does
Connectivity chips inside AI servers
Move
Added Mar 30
Watching
Attach rates on new AI platforms

Astera Labs designs connectivity chips for AI servers. Its main product is the retimer. As data speeds climb, electrical signals degrade over shorter distances, so past a few inches of circuit board they have to be cleaned up and resent. Modern AI servers don't work without them.

What I like most is content per server. Every new chip generation runs faster links, and more of them, so each board needs more of Astera's parts. It has also added switches that connect AI chips to each other and controllers for new memory standards. And it's neutral: it sells into every chip vendor's ecosystem, so I don't have to guess which AI chip wins.

The business looks like a good chip designer should. It has no factories, high margins, and deep work with the biggest cloud companies while they design their systems. Once its parts are designed in, they usually stay for the whole generation.

The risks: a short customer list, a much larger competitor that would happily absorb this function, and a valuation that swings hard on any AI news.

I started the position in February and added on Monday's dip at a much lower price. It is now one of my largest holdings.
