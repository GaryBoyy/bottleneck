June rebalance

Trimming the winners, closing NuScale, and five new positions.

The rotation I planned last week happened today. Here's what moved, checked against the rules.

Sold

  • NuScale, closed at a loss of about 44%, under rule 3. The thesis didn't break, but the firm US order I asked for hasn't come, and the money has nearer-term uses.
  • Trims of Micron, Sandisk, Astera Labs and AAOI, back toward target weights under rule 1. Each sold something up a lot from cost, which is exactly when selling feels worst.

Bought

  • Vertiv, power and cooling equipment, sized toward core.
  • Marvell, custom AI chips and optics, to balance my tilt toward small companies.
  • Planet Labs, satellite imaging, my first space position.
  • One Stop Systems and POET, two small speculative bets.

The net effect: less concentration in names that already won, more exposure to power, cooling and data, and an account back in its intended shape. More trims are likely over the next few days as remaining orders work.

A note for later grading: today's sales locked in gains on ideas that worked. Whether the new buys work can't be judged for months.
