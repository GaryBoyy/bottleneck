Portfolio check-in

A month in: how the account is split, and the risk I'm choosing to take.

A month into this journal, here is the shape of the account.

  • Optics and connectivity is the largest group at about a third of the account. AAOI is up about two-thirds since March and is now my biggest position.
  • Compute and power is about a fifth: CoreWeave, IREN and a small nuclear position, NuScale, that I haven't written up yet.
  • Memory is about 15%, in two positions from last fall. Sandisk is up more than fourfold from my cost, and Micron has nearly doubled.
  • Outside AI is about 8%: antimony miner UAMY and drone maker Ondas.
  • Cash is just under a quarter.

Winners resize a portfolio without asking. AAOI was already a big bet, and four weeks of gains made it a fifth of the account. For now I'm letting it run, but I've started writing rules for when trimming stops being optional.

The risk check: almost everything here is one correlated bet. If AI spending pauses, nearly all of it falls together, and the small diversifiers won't save it. I accept that. The cash is the counterweight.

One discipline test: can I say in two sentences why I own each position and what would make me sell it? Today the answer is yes for all but one. That one gets researched or sold by the end of the month.
