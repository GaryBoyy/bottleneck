Why I bought MRVL

Two AI businesses in one company, at a lagging price.

MRVLMarvell TechnologyChips & networking
What it does
Custom AI chips for cloud companies, plus optical chips
Move
Bought Jun 5
Watching
Custom-chip wins and ramp timing

Marvell has two separate AI businesses in one company, at a price that has lagged the famous names in each.

Custom chips. The largest cloud companies don't want to buy every AI chip from one vendor, so each is designing its own. A cutting-edge chip needs skills few companies have: advanced packaging, high-speed interconnect, and close ties to the top foundries. Marvell is one of only a couple of design partners that can do this at scale, and custom silicon is its fastest-growing business.

Optics. Marvell leads in the signal processors inside high-speed optical modules, the electronic brains of each link. My AAOI post covered why transceiver volumes are booming. Marvell collects on the same boom one layer up, where there are fewer competitors and better margins.

The risks: custom-chip revenue is lumpy and fought over every generation, losing one big program would leave a visible hole, and its main rival is much bigger. The stock is only cheap compared with the AI leaders.

I opened near core size, with room to add if the custom programs deliver. In an account full of small companies betting on one product, a large company with two ways to win adds stability.
