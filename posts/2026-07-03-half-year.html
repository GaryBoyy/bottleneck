Half-year review

The account more than doubled in six months. Here's what did it.

Six months are done. Percentages only, but here's everything.

The headline

The account rose about 116% in the first half, measured on a time-weighted basis so the cash I added in March doesn't inflate it. The S&P 500 rose about 10% and the semiconductor ETF (SMH) about 82%. Cash ended June at about 36% of the account.

Winners

Sandisk is my trade of the year, up roughly 950% from my fall cost even after four rounds of selling. Micron is up about 380% and Astera Labs about 365%. Behind them: AAOI +48%, CoreWeave +26%, UAMY +25%, Vertiv +13%, One Stop +10%, Marvell +9% and IREN +6%.

IREN hides a lesson. The spring lot is up about 36%, while last fall's lot was sold at a loss. Averaging into an improved thesis worked this time. It could just as easily have doubled a mistake.

Losers

NuScale (−44%) and Ondas (−15%) were closed in June. POET is down 13% and Planet Labs is about flat. The June buys are too new to grade.

Lessons

  • Nearly everything that worked was one bet on AI hardware. These results measure a theme more than a skill.
  • The structure worked: core positions compounded and the speculative ones stayed small.
  • Selling is still my weakest muscle. Every trim happened on schedule and every one felt wrong, so the rules should stay strict.

Already in July

The second half started badly. Chip stocks fell hard on July 1 and 2, and the account lost about 9% in two days. I have plenty of cash, and this is the kind of drop I keep it for.
