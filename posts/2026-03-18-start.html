Starting this journal

Why I'm writing down my reasoning, and the rules I'll hold myself to.

This journal is a record of why I make each investment decision, written before I know how it turns out. Looking back, I want to see whether I was right for the right reasons.

The idea behind the account is simple. The biggest spending wave in tech is the buildout of computing for AI: chips, memory, networking, power and cooling. I don't know which AI model or app will win, and I don't need to. They all run on the same hardware, so that's what I own.

The account has three parts:

  • Core positions in companies that sit on a real bottleneck in that supply chain.
  • Small speculative bets that can pay off big and cost little if they fail.
  • A cash reserve, because the best prices usually show up on bad days.

Three rules for this journal. Every number is a percentage: position sizes are a share of the account, and results are gains or losses against cost. I'll post once or twice a week. And when a thesis breaks, I write that post too.

None of this is advice. It's a notebook I'm keeping in public so I can't quietly rewrite it later.
