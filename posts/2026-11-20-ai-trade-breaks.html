MarketsNovember 20, 2026

The day the AI trade broke

Nvidia fell 14%, the Nasdaq erased its 2026 gains, and my account is a quarter below the money I put in.

Today was the worst day for AI stocks since I started this journal. The Nasdaq fell about 5% and wiped out its gains for the year. The S&P 500 fell 3.7% and is now more than 10% below its October high, an official correction. Nvidia fell 14% and is down nearly half from its October peak. The VIX, Wall Street's fear gauge, briefly topped 40.

What hit at once

  • Google's chip orders. Analyst Ming-Chi Kuo reported that Google cut its 2027 orders for Broadcom-designed TPU chips by about 20%. Broadcom fell 12%. Google hasn't commented.
  • Anthropic's IPO. Anthropic listed yesterday in the largest IPO ever, priced at $83 a share, and opened above $111. Today it slid back toward its offer price. If the hottest AI company can't hold a premium, investors start asking what everything else is worth.
  • Nvidia. The CEO's exit and the reported SEC probe are still unresolved.
  • The Fed. Officials sounded ready to raise rates again in December.

Where I stand

My account fell about 13% today, its second-worst day this year after November 5. It's about 25% below the total money I've put in and about 55% below its June high on a time-weighted basis. For 2026 it's up only about 2%.

The Google report is the closest thing yet to the sell signal I set in October: any of the big four lowering its 2027 plans. But it's an analyst's survey, not Google's own guidance, and Google hasn't confirmed it. Broadcom reports on December 9, the same day as the Fed. If Broadcom confirms the cut, the signal has fired, and I'll cut my AI hardware exposure as I said I would.

I'm not selling on a panic day on an unconfirmed report. But I've bought every dip since July and each one went lower. Being this far down makes me want to wait for a bounce before doing anything, and I know that's a bias I need to watch.
