MarketsNovember 12, 2026

Nvidia loses its CEO

Jensen Huang resigned today. I don't own Nvidia, but almost everything I own sells into it.

Nvidia's board announced today that Jensen Huang has resigned as CEO, effective immediately. The board gave no reason. News reports tie it to an investigation into his personal relationships, including one with a woman U.S. intelligence agencies suspect of spying for China, which China denies. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and board member Harvey Jones becomes chair.

The stock fell about 4%, a calm reaction to losing the founder who has run the company for more than three decades. I think the market is still working out what the investigation could touch.

Why it matters to my account

I don't own Nvidia, but most of my companies are its suppliers. SK Hynix and Micron make memory for its chips, Lumentum and AAOI make optics for its systems, and Astera Labs connects the pieces. Huang set the pace of Nvidia's product roadmap and sold it personally to the biggest customers. I'm not worried that demand disappears next quarter. I'm worried that a leadership gap, or a problem with export controls, slows the next two years. Nvidia reports on Tuesday, its first report under an interim CEO.

The rest of the week

The economy is still running hot. October added 165,000 jobs, far above forecasts, and consumer prices rose 0.4% in the month. Traders now fully expect a Fed rate hike on December 9.

My account bounced back this week after last Thursday's drop and is roughly back to the total money I've put in. With cash near 5%, I'm not making new moves.
