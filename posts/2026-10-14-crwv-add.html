Adding to CRWV in a bond scare

The bond market is pricing in serious default risk. I think it's too gloomy, and I bought.

CRWVCoreWeaveCompute & sites
What it does
Rents out AI computing on multi-year contracts
Move
Added Oct 14
Watching
Its bond yields and the terms of its next debt deal

CoreWeave fell 15.5% today and is down about 20% since Friday. The selling started in its bonds. Yields on its 2032 bonds hit a record near 15% this week, and the price of insuring its debt against default now implies something like a 60% chance of default within five years.

In August I wrote that the debt sets my sizing and that I wasn't adding. Today I added anyway, at about 39% below the August high. Here's why I changed my mind.

The business hasn't changed since August. The backlog of signed contracts is still above $100 billion, most of the debt is tied to specific contracts with large customers, and Nvidia has agreed to buy any unused capacity through 2032. Nothing in the quarter so far says customers are walking away. What changed is the price of the debt, and fear about AI borrowing in general. I think a 60% default probability is too pessimistic for a company with that backlog.

The risk is real, though. If CoreWeave has to refinance at 15%, the interest bill eats much of the profit from its contracts, and the stock could fall a lot further. I'm betting the bond market is wrong, and bond markets are often right about debt.

The position is now about 8% of the account. That's the cap. If its bonds keep falling or its next debt deal comes at these yields, I'll treat that as the thesis breaking.

Also today, September inflation came in at 3.7%, above forecasts, with gasoline up 9% in a single month. That makes another Fed hike more likely.
